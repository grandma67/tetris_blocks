    How to Play

    • 🧩 Drag the pieces from the tray onto the board.
    • ➖ Fill a full row or column to clear it.
    • 🔁 Place all pieces to get a new set. Pieces can't be rotated.
    • 💥 Clear several lines at once for big points.
    • 🔥 Keep clearing on consecutive moves to build a combo multiplier.
    • ✨ Empty the whole board for a huge bonus.
    • ☠️ If none of your pieces fits anywhere — game over!

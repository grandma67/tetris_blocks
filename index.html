    How to Play

    • 🧩 Drag the pieces from the tray onto the board.
    • 🎯 Levels: reach the goal score to clear a level. Each new level starts fresh, with a few grey stones in the way.
    • 🎲 Some levels add a twist: 🪨 stones drop in, ⬇ gravity makes blocks fall after clears (cascades!), or a 🧱 stone floor grows a row at a time (fill its gap to clear it). Each level number always plays the same way.
    • ∞ Endless: no goals — just play until you run out of room.
    • ➖ Fill a full row or column to clear it.
    • 🔁 Place all pieces to get a new set. Pieces can't be rotated.
    • 💥 Clear several lines at once for big points.
    • 🔥 Keep clearing on consecutive moves to build a combo multiplier.
    • ✨ Empty the whole board for a huge bonus.
    • ↻ Tap a piece to rotate it — each turn costs a token. Clear 3+ lines at once to earn more.
    • 💣 When a bomb's row or column clears, it blows up everything around it — and other bombs too!
    • ☠️ If none of your pieces fits anywhere, the game ends. In Levels you just retry the same level — your progress is saved.
